# Paper Formatting Specification

This specification is derived from direct inspection and rendering of the prior Operations/Capacity Planning paper `Adithya_Hegde_Capacity_Planning_Research_Paper_FINAL.docx`. It is a layout benchmark, not a content source.

## Verified visual structure

- Page size: standard portrait document with approximately 1-inch margins.
- Professional rectangular page border appears around the page content.
- Body text uses a serif academic typeface consistent with Times New Roman, approximately 12 pt.
- Main body is double-spaced with first-line paragraph indentation.
- Main section headings are numbered and bold, e.g. `1. INTRODUCTION`.
- Subsections use decimal numbering and bold hierarchy, e.g. `1.1 Background` and `1.2 Problem Statement`.
- Abstract is on its own page immediately after the title page.
- Keywords appear at the end of the abstract page in bold label form.
- References begin on a new page with the centered/bold `REFERENCES` heading.
- The title page is centered vertically/horizontally around the PES University identity, project title, student information, and faculty submission information.
- The PES University logo and institutional identification are prominent on the title page.
- Tables and figures are integrated into the body and use numbered labels/captions.
- References use an academic hanging-indent presentation and DOI/URL information.

## Final rendering gate

Before delivery, render the final DOCX to PDF and inspect every page for:

- page-border continuity;
- title-page spacing and logo placement;
- heading numbering and hierarchy;
- double-spacing and paragraph indentation;
- tables fitting inside margins;
- figure/caption/source-label alignment;
- no orphaned headings or stranded captions;
- no clipped text or images;
- no unexpected blank pages;
- consistent page density;
- references with consistent hanging indentation and no overflow;
- correct page count and pagination;
- consistency between DOCX and PDF output.
